# Frequently asked questions

Sample content for a fictional business, Bramblewood Dog Spa.

## Do you groom cats?

No, we only groom dogs.

## Can I stay with my dog during the groom?

For the safety of all dogs, owners wait outside the grooming room. For puppy introduction visits, one owner may stay.

## Do you use cages?

Dogs rest in roomy, open-top pens between steps. We do not use heated cage dryers.

## What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash and all major debit and credit cards. We do not accept cheques.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available in the salon in any amount from $25 (sample amount) and never expire.

## Do you offer mobile grooming?

No, all grooming happens in our salon.
