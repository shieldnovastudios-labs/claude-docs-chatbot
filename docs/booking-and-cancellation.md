# Booking and cancellation policy

Sample content for a fictional business, Bramblewood Dog Spa.

## How to book

Book online through the booking link on our website, call 555-0142, or email hello@example.com. Please tell us your dog's name, breed, approximate weight and the service you want.

New clients are asked to arrive 10 minutes early for their first visit to fill in a short form.

## Deposits

We do not take a deposit for regular bookings. Bookings for three or more dogs at the same time need a $20 deposit per dog (sample amount), which is taken off the final bill.

## Cancellations and rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. You can do this online, by phone or by email.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and missed appointments without notice, are charged 50% of the booked service price.

## Late arrivals

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the service or move your appointment to another time.

## Pick-up

We will text you when your dog is ready. Please pick up within 30 minutes of our message. Dogs not collected by closing time are charged a late pick-up fee of $15 (sample amount).
