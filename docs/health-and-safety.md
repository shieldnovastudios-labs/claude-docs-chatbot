# Health and safety

Sample content for a fictional business, Bramblewood Dog Spa.

## Vaccinations

All dogs must be up to date on rabies and distemper (DHPP) vaccinations. Please bring proof of vaccination to your first visit, or email it to hello@example.com before the appointment.

## Fleas

If we find fleas during the groom, we will add a flea shampoo to the service and let you know at pick-up.

## Senior dogs and health conditions

Please tell us about any health conditions, injuries or anxiety when you book so we can plan breaks and a calm space. For medical questions, please ask your veterinarian.

## Aggressive or very anxious dogs

Our groomers will stop a groom if continuing would be unsafe for your dog or our staff. You will only be charged for the work that was completed.

## Products

We use tearless, fragrance-light shampoos. If your dog has a sensitive skin product from your vet, you are welcome to bring it.
