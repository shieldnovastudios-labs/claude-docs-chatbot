# Services and prices

Sample content for a fictional business, Bramblewood Dog Spa. All prices are SAMPLE prices for this demo.

## Full groom

A full groom includes a bath with gentle shampoo, blow-dry, haircut to your chosen style, nail trim, ear cleaning and a finishing spritz.

- Small dogs (up to 10 kg): $55 (sample price)
- Medium dogs (10–25 kg): $70 (sample price)
- Large dogs (over 25 kg): $90 (sample price)

A full groom takes about 2 to 3 hours depending on size and coat.

## Bath and tidy

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out, nail trim and a tidy around the face, feet and rear. No full haircut.

- Small dogs: $35 (sample price)
- Medium dogs: $45 (sample price)
- Large dogs: $60 (sample price)

## Add-ons

- Nail trim on its own (walk-in, no appointment needed): $15 (sample price)
- Teeth brushing: $10 (sample price)
- De-shedding treatment: $20 (sample price)
- Flea shampoo: $12 (sample price)

## Puppy introduction

For puppies under 6 months: a short, gentle first visit with a bath, brush and nail trim so they get used to grooming. $30 (sample price), about 45 minutes.

## Matted coats

Heavily matted coats may need extra time. We will always call you before charging a matting fee, which is $10 per 15 minutes (sample price).
